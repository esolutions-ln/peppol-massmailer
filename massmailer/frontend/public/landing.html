Two rails, one invoice

Not every customer is on the PEPPOL network yet. Deliver to both.

InvoiceDirect treats email and PEPPOL AS4 as equal, first-class delivery rails — not a network mode with an email afterthought. Every recipient gets the rail that actually reaches them.

@
Rail A

Email delivery

  • Fiscalised PDF attached, sent via SMTP with your own sender identity
  • Custom Thymeleaf templates per organisation
  • Bulk campaigns, single sends, or ERP-triggered dispatch
  • Bounces and failures retried automatically
P
Rail B

PEPPOL AS4 network

  • 4-corner model routing with live SMP/SML lookup
  • UBL BIS Billing 3.0, XML-DSIG signed, XML-Enc encrypted
  • Certificate lifecycle managed for you, per organisation
  • Falls back to email automatically if the network can't be reached

Usage-based billing, tiered by volume

03

Multi-tenant by design

Run one platform, many organisations — each with its own senders, templates, PEPPOL certificates, and rate profile.

04

Hexagonal ERP adapters

A single port interface behind every ERP connector, so adding the next accounting system doesn't touch delivery logic.

05

Retry and fallback, automatic

A failed PEPPOL delivery doesn't sit in a queue — it drops to email. A bounced email gets retried on schedule.

06

Webhooks for every status

Sent, delivered, bounced, fiscal-check-failed — pushed to your systems the moment they happen, signed for verification.
